A destination wedding venue in the Italian countryside, one hour from Rome

Campo Antico is a family-run farm venue in Tuscia, between Rome and Florence. Your guests stay with us, eat from our own kitchen and spend the days around your wedding in Rome, Florence and the Tuscan wine country.

Questions couples ask us

How far is Campo Antico from Rome?

About one hour. Orte has direct trains from Rome, and Rome Fiumicino airport is about 90 minutes by car. We run the shuttles for your guests.

How much does a destination wedding at Campo Antico cost?

For 60 guests a full wedding week starts from €22,460: reception, welcome dinner, pool brunch, four nights' exclusive use of the farm and shuttles. The reception alone starts from €140 per guest.

Who pays for what at a destination wedding?

Usually the couple pays for the wedding and the events for everyone, such as the welcome dinner and the brunch. Guests pay for their flights, rooms and trips. Many couples add a gift, like a night at the hotel or a cooking class for everyone.

Can we have a legal wedding in Italy?

Yes. Most couples from abroad hold a symbolic ceremony at the farm and the legal part at home. If you want a civil wedding in Italy, we help with the paperwork and the town hall.

Where do our guests sleep?

Up to 120 guests between the farm, our Hotel Aquila 8 km away and partner hotels nearby. Rooms at Hotel Aquila cost €130 for two, €170 for three and €210 for four per night.

How many guests can you host?

From small weddings of 30 guests up to 200 at the farm, and up to 400 in our second venue.

When is the best season?

From May to September, with the pool open from April to October. Each date goes to the first couple who pays the deposit.

How do we book?

Send us your date and number of guests. We reply with your proposal within two days. The date is yours when the 30% deposit arrives; the balance is due 30 days before the wedding.

Do you work with wedding planners?

Yes. Our prices are the same whether a couple comes directly or through a planner, and the planner's commission is already included.

For wedding planners and travel agents

Same prices for couples who come directly or through you, commission already included, your own area to build proposals from our price list, and a site visit with dinner and a night on us.
