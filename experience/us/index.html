From the Etruscans to Antinori

The Etruscans were among the first to make wine in Italy, right here in Tuscia. Every day of the journey weaves the same three threads: what you see in the morning, what you drink at lunch, what you cook at night.

Art & history

Rome with a private guide, Florence overnight, Civita di Bagnoregio on its cliff, wine cellars carved into tufa stone.

Wine

Lunch at Antinori nel Chianti Classico in Bargino, a day with the Cotarella family, tastings chosen to match what you cook.

The Italian kitchen

Hands-on classes with a working chef: fresh pasta, the market, a Tuscan menu, and a gala dinner to close.

You know the name from Napa Valley. Come and see where it began.

Twenty-six generations of Tuscan winemaking, in a cellar built into the Chianti hillside. Then Florence by night, and back to the farm to cook the dishes that belong with those wines.

Never rushed

Five days out, four slow days at the farm. Cook in the morning, swim in the afternoon, eat together every evening.

Questions travelers ask us

How much does the Italian Experience tour cost?

From €4,900 (about $5,600) per person sharing a double room, for 10 days and 9 nights with meals, guides, tickets, transfers and insurance. A single room is €690 more. Flights are not included.

How big are the groups?

Up to 16 guests, with a tour leader always with the group. Departures are guaranteed from 6 guests; if the minimum isn't reached, your deposit is refunded in full.

Where do we stay?

At Campo Antico, our family farm near Orte, one hour from Rome, with one night in Florence. Rooms are simple and rustic, with a pool open from April to October.

Do I need to know how to cook?

No. The classes are hands-on and relaxed, led by a working chef, and you eat what you make.

Which wineries do you visit?

Antinori nel Chianti Classico at Bargino and the Cotarella family winery, plus tastings of Lazio wines matched to what you cook.

How do I book?

Choose a departure and pay a 30% deposit to secure your place; the balance is due 30 days before departure. Private dates are available on request.
